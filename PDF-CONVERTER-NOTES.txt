PDF converter baseline: native text/table reconstruction + scanned hybrid reconstruction.

Native/text PDFs:
- PyMuPDF text with coordinate-aware grouping
- pdfplumber table extraction
- real Word tables
- repeated header/footer preservation
- compact Arial formatting and source page breaks

Scanned/image PDFs:
- render actual PDF page to a stable raster
- preserve non-table artwork as page image segments
- detect prominent ruled tables
- reconstruct detected tables as real editable Word tables
- retain photos, signatures, logos and handwriting as image artwork
- OCR remains available for TXT/HTML

The scanned HSSC regression case is HSSC(3).pdf. Its page 2 marks grid is rebuilt as an editable Word table while the surrounding certificate remains visually preserved.
