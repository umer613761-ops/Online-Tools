Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Your privacy matters to me. This Privacy Policy explains how ToolNest handles information when you use the website and its online tools.

Information You Provide

Most ToolNest tools are designed to work without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

If you contact me or choose to provide information, I may receive the information you include in your message, such as your name or email address. I use that information to respond to you or address your request.

Files and Tool Data

Some ToolNest tools require you to select or enter content in order to perform a task. Where a tool processes your files or text in your browser, that content stays on your device and is not sent to me.

ToolNest is designed to process content in your browser whenever possible.

Cookies

ToolNest may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and help the website work properly. Google AdSense may also use cookies to help display and measure advertisements.

Advertising

ToolNest uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to help show and measure ads. For more information about how Google handles information, you can review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

ToolNest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact me so I can address the issue.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as ToolNest changes or new features are added. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how ToolNest handles information, please use the contact option provided on the website.
